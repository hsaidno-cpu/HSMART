## 4. Ratification procedure

1. Complete GOV-01 and GOV-02 appointments in the Role Appointment Register.
2. Complete the minimum Product, Architecture, Engineering and Security role appointments or record formally authorized role-combination exceptions.
3. Have the appointed Sponsor/Accountable Owner and Project Owner review Charter v1.0.0, MVP Decision Record v1.1.0 and the initial risk register.
4. Record one of the three decision options in section 3, along with approver identity, basis of authority, date, conditions and evidence reference.
5. If conditions require normative changes, create or update the appropriate document in a reviewed PR. Do not silently rewrite the adopted baseline.
6. Update the Charter's status and ratification table only after the approval evidence is durable and read back.
7. Mark ratification complete only after the canonical repository reflects the same decision.

A chat instruction may record project direction when the authorized project decision-maker explicitly identifies themselves and their authority. It is not a cryptographic signature or a substitute for any legal instrument required by law or contract.

## 5. Decision rights and safeguards

1. Repository ownership or authorship does not automatically prove appointment as Project Sponsor or Project Owner.
2. The assistant cannot ratify the Charter on behalf of a human authority.
3. No legal Foundation or Commercial Operator entity is created by this record.
4. No IP assignment, trademark, contributor agreement, publisher contract or commercial obligation is created by this record.
5. If adoption is conditional, each condition must have an owner, due condition and acceptance evidence.
6. If the authorized approver returns the Charter for amendment, the current v1.0.0 remains an unratified baseline until a revised version is approved.
