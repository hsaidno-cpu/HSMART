# HSMART Legal and Commercial Readiness Register

**ID:** HSMART-LEGAL-001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** INTERNAL ISSUE REGISTER — EXTERNAL LEGAL READINESS NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Functional delegate:** GOV-08 Legal / Commercial Research (AI; not legal counsel)

HSMART is treated as an unincorporated founder-led personal project for internal governance. GOV-01's project authority does not create a company, foundation, partnership, statutory office, IP title or power to bind third parties.

GOV-08 may research public requirements, identify issues, prepare checklists and draft questions/documents for qualified review. It is not a lawyer, tax adviser, regulator, notary, statutory representative or signatory. It must not issue a binding legal opinion or label an external commitment legally approved.

Before a commercial/public launch, obtain appropriate qualified review of:
- entity and operator structure, jurisdiction and authority;
- ownership and chain-of-title for HSMART code, designs, trademarks and contributor work;
- third-party Skill/plugin/model/API licenses and redistribution/hosting rights;
- publisher and consumer terms, warranties, liability, support, takedown and appeal;
- privacy/data controller-processor roles, data location, retention, transfer, security and incident duties;
- tax, invoicing, payments, refunds, chargebacks and accounting.

Do not accept publishers, distribute third-party artifacts, process customer data, collect payment or make service-level commitments until the relevant rights, authority and evidence have been verified. Retain sources and dates; recheck current law and platform terms before use.

**Disposition:** issue register only. No legal entity formation, IP title opinion, regulatory compliance certification, tax approval or commercial terms approval is claimed.
