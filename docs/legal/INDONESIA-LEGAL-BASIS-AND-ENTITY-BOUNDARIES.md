**Authority:** GOV-01; internal HSMART project governance only  
**Research jurisdiction:** Indonesia

## 1. Decision boundary

HSMART is currently treated as an unincorporated founder-led personal project. The internal governance model has one human authority, GOV-01. GOV-02 through GOV-12 are AI Functional Delegates. This internal allocation is valid as a project operating convention but does not create a legal entity, statutory authority, professional qualification, IP title or third-party authority.

**Critical distinction:** AI delegation can allocate internal research, design, engineering, operational planning and assurance functions. It cannot turn an AI into a natural person where Indonesian law requires a natural-person Director, Foundation organ, notary, lawyer, tax adviser or authorized signatory.

## 2. Legal form options

### 2.1 Unincorporated personal project

This is the current posture. GOV-01 may manage internal project decisions and resources actually controlled by GOV-01. This status does not automatically permit regulated or commercial operations, bind collaborators, transfer third-party rights, or represent HSMART as a separate company or Foundation.

### 2.2 Yayasan

The Indonesian Foundations Law is UU 16/2001 as amended by UU 28/2004. A Yayasan is a statutory legal entity with a deed/registration/approval process, separated assets and legally specified organs. HSMART's AI role registry is not a substitute for the statutory Pembina, Pengurus and Pengawas, and cannot itself establish legal personality. Formation and organ appointments require qualified Indonesian notarial/legal work and real persons with the required legal capacity.

### 2.3 Perseroan Terbatas (PT)

The PT framework includes UU 40/2007, as amended including by UU 6/2023. Article 93 governs natural-person eligibility for Directors. An AI GOV profile cannot be appointed as a PT Director or sign for the company. The one-human internal HSMART model can coexist with a PT only if the PT's statutory organs and signatory authority are separately and lawfully staffed by actual natural persons. Those external appointments do not need to become internal HSMART GOV roles.

## 4. Practical stop conditions

1. Do not accept a third-party capability for hosted distribution until the publisher's identity, authority and exact distribution/execution rights are recorded.
2. Do not process personal/customer data until HSMART's operator/entity role, lawful basis, notice, purpose, retention, security and processor terms are approved.
3. Do not collect payment, list paid plans or promise service levels until entity/operator authority, tax, consumer terms, payments and liabilities are reviewed.
4. Do not describe HSMART as a Yayasan, PT, Foundation, Commercial Operator or regulated PSE until the relevant formation/registration evidence exists.
5. Do not use an AI delegate as legal adviser, notary, statutory officer, Director, organ member or signatory.
