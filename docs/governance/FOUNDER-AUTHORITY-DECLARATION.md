## 1. Declaration and purpose

The declarant identifies himself as the founder and project initiator of HSMART and expressly establishes this declaration as the internal authority basis for project-level governance while HSMART remains a personal, founder-led project without a registered legal entity.

This instrument records internal project authority. It does not create a foundation, company, partnership, association, agency, trust, or other legal person. It does not convert HSMART into a legal entity or confer authority that the declarant does not independently possess.

## 2. Authority basis

The basis is the declarant's explicit self-declaration in the HSMART project-governance instruction dated 2026-11-22, together with the declarant's stated role as founder and project initiator.

For internal project governance, the declarant may make decisions for HSMART only to the extent that the relevant project activity, account, asset, funds, work product, and permission are actually controlled by or lawfully available to the declarant.

Repository ownership, account access, authorship, or this declaration alone must not be treated as proof of ownership of every contribution, third-party asset, brand, domain, dataset, or intellectual-property right associated with HSMART.

## 4. Authorized internal decisions

Within the limits in section 5, GOV-01 may:

1. Set and revise the strategic direction, objectives, priorities, and phase boundaries of the personal HSMART project.
2. Approve internal project scope, roadmap priorities, and project-level decisions within the approved governance process.
3. Approve or reject project proposals and internal baseline decisions after required product, architecture, engineering, security, and risk reviews.
4. Allocate the declarant's own time, work, and resources, and resources for which the declarant has explicit lawful authority.
5. Assign project responsibilities to willing participants through explicit acceptance and documented delegation; this declaration does not compel another person to participate.
6. Establish internal policies, escalation paths, review gates, and project records.
7. Accept or defer project-level risks only within the declarant's authority and without overriding required independent security, legal, privacy, contractual, or platform reviews.
8. Pause, reduce, or terminate the personal project.

GOV-01 may exercise D2 approval within this internal scope. D3 authority is limited to internal project-governance decisions and cannot create external legal or commercial obligations.

## 5. Explicit limits and reserved matters

This authority does **not** authorize GOV-01 to:

- Represent HSMART as an incorporated company, foundation, registered organization, or other legal entity.
- Sign, vary, or terminate contracts on behalf of a non-existent HSMART entity or another person or organization.
- Bind a co-founder, contributor, investor, customer, publisher, platform, employer, or other third party without that party's valid authorization.
- Commit another person's funds, labor, intellectual property, data, account, or other assets.
- Claim ownership of third-party or contributor work without a valid assignment, license, or other applicable rights instrument.
- Create an IP assignment, trademark transfer, publisher agreement, employment relationship, partnership, investment obligation, debt, guarantee, or other external commitment merely through this declaration.
- Override applicable law, contract terms, platform policies, data-protection requirements, security controls, or another owner's authorization.
- Approve a material security exception, unsafe production operation, or destructive production-data action without the required independent review and safeguards.

Any external contract, legal entity, ownership/IP, licensing, employment, tax, privacy, publisher, fundraising, or commercial decision must be separately reviewed and executed by the legally authorized person or entity, with qualified professional advice where appropriate.

## 6. Resource and financial boundary

GOV-01 may decide how to use personal resources that the declarant controls. Any spending must remain within funds actually available to and authorized for the declarant. No project budget, external funding, cloud procurement, payment commitment, or financial capacity is implied by this declaration.

Paid commitments, recurring subscriptions, purchases, or third-party service obligations must be explicitly reviewed before they are made. This document is not a purchase order, funding commitment, or delegation of another party's budget.

## 7. Intellectual property, contributions, and project assets

This declaration does not decide who owns every HSMART-related work product.

- The declarant retains only those rights that belong to the declarant under applicable law and agreements.
- Each contributor retains rights unless they are validly licensed or assigned under a separate agreement.
- Third-party code, datasets, fonts, designs, trademarks, model assets, and other materials remain subject to their own licenses and terms.
- Before accepting external contributions or commercializing shared work, HSMART must document provenance, permissions, contributor terms, and any required legal review.
- Repository administration is an access-control fact, not conclusive proof of intellectual-property title.

## 8. Governance safeguards

1. GOV-01 does not automatically become GOV-02, GOV-03, GOV-04, GOV-05, GOV-06, or any other role.
2. Every additional role requires an explicit appointment or documented role-combination decision.
3. Security-sensitive changes should receive review independent from the implementer wherever practical. Where the team cannot provide independence, the conflict and compensating external review must be recorded.
4. GOV-01 cannot waive legal, security, privacy, safety, or third-party authorization requirements merely by accepting project risk.
5. Material decisions must be recorded with rationale, scope, alternatives, risk, approval date, and evidence.
6. A change in project structure, legal status, ownership, or control requires this declaration and the role register to be reviewed and superseded where necessary.
